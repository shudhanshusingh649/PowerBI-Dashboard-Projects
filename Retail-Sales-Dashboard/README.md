# Retail Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Retail Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze retail sales performance, customer behavior, product category performance, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard provides a clear and visual overview of important business metrics and allows users to interact with the data using filters such as Gender and Product Category.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Track total sales and total quantity sold.
- Monitor the total number of customers.
- Analyze Average Order Value (AOV).
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Understand sales performance by gender.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### Total Sales by Product Category

A bar chart is used to compare total sales across three product categories:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Beauty

Electronics shows the highest sales among the displayed product categories.

### Total Sales by Gender

This visualization shows sales performance based on customer gender and helps understand purchasing behavior across gender segments.

### Quantity by Product Category

A pie chart represents the distribution of total quantity sold across the different product categories.

### Total Sales by Month

A line chart displays the monthly sales trend from January to December, helping identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### Total Quantity by Product Category

A donut chart shows the percentage distribution of total quantity sold across different product categories.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to analyze the data based on:

- **Gender**
- **Product Category**

These filters make it easier to explore specific customer segments and product categories.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronics has the highest sales among the displayed product categories.
- Clothing and Beauty also contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating different sales patterns across months.
- Product category analysis helps identify which categories contribute more to sales and quantity.
- Gender-based analysis provides insights into customer purchasing behavior.
- The combination of KPIs, charts, and filters makes the dashboard useful for quick business analysis.
