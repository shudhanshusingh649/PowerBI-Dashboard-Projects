# Super Store Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Super Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, quantity sold, customer segments, payment modes, shipping modes, product categories, sub-categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard also includes a 15-day sales forecasting analysis to help understand potential future sales trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track total quantity sold.
- Understand sales performance across different customer segments.
- Analyze sales by payment mode.
- Compare sales performance across different regions and states.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Understand sales performance by shipping mode.
- Analyze sales performance across different cities.
- Provide a 15-day sales forecast for future trend analysis.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- The dashboard provides a clear view of overall sales and profitability.
- Customer segments can be compared to identify major contributors to sales.
- Payment mode analysis helps understand customer purchasing preferences.
- Regional and state-level analysis helps identify high-performing markets.
- Category and sub-category analysis helps identify products contributing significantly to sales.
- Monthly sales and profit trends help identify changes in business performance over time.
- Shipping mode analysis provides insights into preferred delivery methods.
- City-level analysis helps identify locations with higher sales contribution.
- The forecasting page provides an estimated view of upcoming sales trends based on historical sales data.
